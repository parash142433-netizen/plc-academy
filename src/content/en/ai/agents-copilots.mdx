# Agents & Copilots

AI assistants and autonomous agents as engineering tools: Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT and their practical application in automation.

**AI agents** and **copilots** represent the next step in artificial intelligence: they don't just answer questions — they execute complex tasks autonomously. GitHub Copilot writes code in real time, Claude manages multi-file projects, and agents chain tools to solve problems without human intervention.
