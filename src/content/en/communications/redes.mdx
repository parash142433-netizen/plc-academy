# Industrial Ethernet Networks

Concepts, simulation and analysis of Ethernet networks applied to industrial environments: PROFINET, EtherNet/IP and more.

Industrial Ethernet networks are the backbone of communication between PLCs, HMIs, SCADAs and field devices in modern plants. Protocols such as **PROFINET** (Siemens) and **EtherNet/IP** (Rockwell) use standard Ethernet adapted for real-time critical environments.
