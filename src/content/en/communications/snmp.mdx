# SNMP

Simple Network Management Protocol — Monitoring and management of industrial network devices.

SNMP is the standard protocol for monitoring and managing network devices: switches, routers, industrial gateways and communication systems. It allows collecting performance metrics, receiving alerts (traps) and configuring devices remotely from monitoring systems.
