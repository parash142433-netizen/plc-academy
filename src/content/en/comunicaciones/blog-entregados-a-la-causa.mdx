# Committed to the Cause

Pedro Pagán Pallarés Jan 02, 2023

Corporate

![Claude-generated illustration: two programmers coding on the moon](./assets/generated/moon-programmers.svg)

We start 2023 with the inauguration of the new portal at www.plc-hmi-scadas.com/blog/. In this new space, we aim to further expand our ability to continue sharing content in a didactic and entertaining way!

![](./assets/images/blog-entregados-a-la-causa/Logo-PHS-1.png)

_New Logo_

In this new stage, we will be part of the team, _Jose Manuel Luque_ - founder - and _Pedro Pagán_ - collaborator and friend -. Each of us will try to contribute our grain of sand, generating the necessary synergies to carry out this journey in a rewarding way.

In this new version of the website, we will continue sharing content about the classics - **PLC, HMI, and SCADAS** - as well as new systems and technologies that are starting to gain traction today.

Of course, this new site will have a specific section for **artificial intelligence** technologies. We will conduct experiments and seek applications and usefulness for these new tools. You can visit it at [/blog/IA/](#/a/en%2Fblog-ia).

![Claude-generated illustration: two programmers coding on the moon](./assets/generated/moon-programmers.svg)

Image automatically generated by DALL-E 2. _"Two programmer boys, sitting on the moon"_

Of course, we will continue to expand content on the automation tools we know best, delving into the most promising and innovative ones.

We hope you enjoy this new space and that you send us all the comments you deem appropriate, so we can continue improving together in this wonderful world of knowledge.

As Jose Manuel usually says:

> Here we are, committed to the cause.

Happy New Year to everyone, let's get started.

#### Pedro Pagán Pallarés

Automation and industrial organization engineer specialized in data and applied AI.
