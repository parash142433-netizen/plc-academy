Articles on configuring Omron NB3Q panels.

The **Omron NB series** (NB3Q, NB5Q, NB7W, NB10W) are colour TFT touch panels programmed with **NB-Designer**. They offer direct communication with Omron PLCs (CJ, CP1, NJ/NX series) via serial ports and Ethernet.

NB-Designer allows creating screens with graphic objects, alarms, recipes, and multi-protocol communication. NB panels also support communication with Siemens PLCs (S7-200/300) via PPI and Ethernet protocols, making them a versatile option in multi-vendor environments.

Here you will find examples of communication between the Omron NB3Q and S7-200, as well as project transfer to the panel.
