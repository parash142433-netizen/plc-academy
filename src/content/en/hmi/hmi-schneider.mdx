Articles on configuring Schneider (Telemecanique) panels: Magelis and Vijeo Designer.

**Schneider Electric** (Telemecanique) HMI panels comprise the **Magelis** range, programmed with **XBT-L1000** (for the legacy XBT-P panels) and **Vijeo Designer** (for the STO, STU, Smart iPC and Compact iPC series).

**Vijeo Designer** V6.2 allows configuring communication with Schneider PLCs (Modbus, Uni-Telway), Siemens (S7-1200 via Ethernet), Rockwell (SoftLogix/Ethernet) and other manufacturers through OPC drivers. It includes a screen editor, alarm management, recipes, and a built-in simulator.

In this section we cover co-simulation with Unity Pro, Vijeo Runtime configuration on PC, and integration with different PLCs.
