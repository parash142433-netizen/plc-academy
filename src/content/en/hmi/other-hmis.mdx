# Other HMIs

Section dedicated to HMI panels from other manufacturers: Delta, B&R and more.

Section dedicated to **HMI panels from other manufacturers**. Even if tutorials are not yet available for all of them, we will at least provide information on the brand, the programming software used and other relevant details.

**Delta Electronics** with their DOP-B Screen Editor offers affordable panels with good performance, frequently used in OEM machines. **B&R Automation Studio** integrates HMI and PLC programming in a single environment, allowing advanced visualizations with their HTML5-based mappView technology.

We will continue expanding this section with new manufacturers and technologies as we work with them.
