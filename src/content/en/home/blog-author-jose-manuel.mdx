# Jose Manuel Luque

Founder · Lead Author · Industrial Automation Specialist

Barcelona, Spain

## About me

I'm Jose Manuel Luque, I live in Barcelona and I'm the founder of **PLC-HMI-SCADAS**. My professional career has taken me through several fields within the industry, but I've now spent over **14 years** immersed in the world of industrial automation. It's what I'm passionate about.

I'm completely passionate about systems. I love digging into the details, understanding how things work on the inside, and getting to the bottom of every subject I take on. Whether it's a PLC, a SCADA system, a communication protocol or a full integration project, if there's more to understand, that's where I'll be.

In July **2012** I decided to create this space to share what I was learning along the way. What started as a personal project has grown steadily over time, and I'm still here, day after day, because every day brings something new.

I firmly believe that working in harmony and with the right attitude is a privilege. Being able to contribute, share and keep learning alongside other professionals is something I value enormously. Here we are, fully committed to the cause.

## My vision

I believe in sharing what I know. It's that simple. Industrial automation is a vast and ever-evolving field, and the best way to grow is to do it alongside others.

If you'd like to make a suggestion, have any questions, or want to contribute content, feel free to reach out through the contact form. It's always welcome.
