# Databases

Configuration and connection of SQL databases applied to SCADA systems and industrial historians.

SCADA systems and industrial historians rely on robust databases for storing process data, alarms and events. Here you will find configuration guides for **SQL Server**, **Oracle**, **MySQL** and **ODBC** connector setup needed for integration with SCADA/HMI software.
