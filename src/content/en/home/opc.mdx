# OPC Servers

OPC (OLE for Process Control) — Standards and specifications for industrial communications in Runtime.

![OPC](./assets/images/opc/OPC.jpg)

OPC was born in 1996. It is a series of standards and specifications for industrial communications in Runtime. Over the years, several specifications have been released: OPC DA v.1, OPC DA v.2, OPC DA v.3, OPC XML-DA and OPC UA.
