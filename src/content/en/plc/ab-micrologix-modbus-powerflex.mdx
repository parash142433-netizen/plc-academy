First of all, I would like to dedicate this practice to Miguel Custodio. As we have seen before, the [Micrologix can be the Master in a Modbus network](#/a/en%2Frslogix500-micrologix-master-modbus), after that first test with the simulator, today we are going to carry it out but with a PowerFlex, we are going to control our drive via Modbus.

The first thing we do is configure the communication parameters, just as we did before.

![](./assets/images/ab-micrologix-modbus-powerflex/Micrologix_Modbus_PowerFlex_01.png)

The values we want to write/read to our drive will be done through MSG Messages, where each of them points to an address, depending on what we want to achieve.

![](./assets/images/ab-micrologix-modbus-powerflex/Micrologix_Modbus_PowerFlex_03.png)

An essential requirement is to know the addresses of the registers we want to access, whether to read or write; for this, I am attaching a PDF with information that cannot be overlooked. Here is an example of the Setup of the main Message.

![](./assets/images/ab-micrologix-modbus-powerflex/Micrologix_Modbus_PowerFlex_05.png)
