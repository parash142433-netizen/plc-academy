# Other PLCs

Articles on other programmable logic controllers: ABB, GE Fanuc, CodeSys and B&R Automation Studio.

This section is dedicated to **other programmable logic controllers** we have had less contact with, but we take the opportunity to document our hands-on experience and what we learn along the way.

**ABB** with their Automation Builder and Control Builder (AC500, AC800), **GE Fanuc** with Versa Pro for the PLC 90-30 and VersaMax, **CodeSys** as a hardware-independent IEC 61131-3 development platform (used by many manufacturers), and **B&R Automation Studio** as an integrated programming and simulation environment.

All these manufacturers offer valid and interesting solutions to the industrial automation market. We will continue expanding this section as we work with new equipment and technologies.
