Articles on programming and configuration of Allen Bradley PLCs, from PLC-5 to CompactLogix/ControlLogix.

**Allen Bradley**, a brand of **Rockwell Automation**, is the market leader in automation in the Americas. Their controller range spans from the classic PLC-5 and SLC-500 to the modern CompactLogix and ControlLogix, programmed with **RSLogix / Studio 5000**.

The **RSLinx** software is the central piece of the Rockwell ecosystem: any communication with an Allen Bradley controller requires configuring a driver in RSLinx (DF1, Ethernet/IP, DH+, ControlNet). Depending on the license (Lite, Professional, Gateway), additional functionalities such as OPC/DDE server are available.

The Logix architecture (CompactLogix, ControlLogix, SoftLogix, FlexLogix) offers unified programming with tags, produced/consumed modules, Add-On Instructions, and a powerful alarm system. In this section we cover everything from basic communication setup to simulations, firmware management, and advanced programming.
