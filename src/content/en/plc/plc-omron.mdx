Articles on programming Omron PLCs with SYSWIN and CX-Programmer.

**Omron** is one of the major Japanese automation manufacturers. Their PLC range includes the compact **CPM/CP1**, the modular **CJ** series, and the rack-mounted **CS** series, programmed with **SYSWIN** and **CX-Programmer** (CX-One).

CX-Programmer, part of the CX-One suite, supports programming in LD (Ladder), FB (Function Block) and ST (Structured Text). The CX-Simulator allows testing programs without hardware. Omron PLCs are known for their Host Link communication, Modbus serial communication, and CompoBus/D (DeviceNet) and EtherCAT networks.

In this section you will find examples of PC-to-PLC communication, simulation with CX-Simulator, and configuration of specific functions such as the FIFO instruction.
