Articles on programming Schneider PLCs: Twido, Modicon with Unity Pro / EcoStruxure and SoMachine.

**Schneider Electric** (formerly Telemecanique) offers a broad range of controllers: from the compact **Twido** and **M221/M241**, to the powerful modular **Modicon Premium** and **Quantum**, programmed with **Unity Pro / EcoStruxure Control Expert**.

Software evolution went from TwidoSuite for compact controllers, through PL7 (for the TSX Micro/Premium), Unity Pro (now EcoStruxure Control Expert) for the Modicon M340, Premium and Quantum, to SoMachine (now EcoStruxure Machine Expert) for the M241/M251 range.

Communication protocols include Modbus RTU/TCP as a native standard, CANOpen for field networks, and Ethernet/IP. Schneider's OPC Server (OFS — OPC Factory Server) enables integration with third-party SCADA systems. Here you will find guides on simulation, communications, and programming with these tools.
