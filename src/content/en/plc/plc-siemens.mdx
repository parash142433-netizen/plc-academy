Articles on programming and configuration of Siemens PLCs from the S5 to the S7-1500.

**Siemens Simatic** PLCs have shaped the evolution of industrial automation in Europe and worldwide. From the legendary S5 (1980s) to the current S7-1200/1500 platform with **TIA Portal**, Siemens has maintained a dominant position in the industry.

The S5 series pioneered programming with Step 5 and TTY/RS-232 communication. The S7-200 family with Microwin offered compact solutions for small machines. The S7-300/400 with Simatic Manager (Step 7) became the standard for mid-range and high-end applications, with field buses like MPI, Profibus DP and Ethernet communication (ISO/TCP, PROFINET).

With the arrival of **TIA Portal (Totally Integrated Automation)**, Siemens unified PLC, HMI and drive programming in a single environment. The S7-1200 and S7-1500 integrate a web server, native OPC UA communication, and advanced diagnostic tools. In this section you will find practical guides for all generations.
