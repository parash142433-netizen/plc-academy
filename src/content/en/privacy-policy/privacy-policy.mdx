## Purposes

*   Providing and maintaining the website.
*   Responding to communications sent by users.
*   Measuring, in aggregate form, the use of pages and resources to improve technical content.
*   Enabling full analytics with Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity only when analytics is accepted.

## First-party aggregate measurement

The website performs first-party technical measurement without cookies or persistent identifiers. Counters are aggregated by day, page, event, target, allowed value and general device type. Full IP addresses, User-ID, free text, URL parameters and advertising information are not stored.

## Consent-based analytics

If you accept analytics, Google Analytics 4 is enabled with `analytics_storage` granted while Google's advertising purposes remain denied. Microsoft Clarity may also be enabled for heatmaps and session recordings, with `ad_Storage` denied and `analytics_Storage` granted.

Behavioural advertising, remarketing, Google Signals, User-ID and cross-domain measurement are not enabled from this implementation.

## Providers and transfers

The third-party analytics providers are Google Analytics 4 (Google) and Microsoft Clarity (Microsoft). Their use may involve processing by international providers. Before publishing this policy, the data-processing agreements, current terms and applicable transfer safeguards must be reviewed and documented.

## Retention

First-party aggregate measurement is retained for up to 395 days. The technical consent cookie lasts approximately 183 days. Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity retention periods must be configured in their panels and documented here.

## Withdrawing consent

You can withdraw or change consent from the **Cookie settings** link in the footer. After withdrawal, the system returns analytics to denied state and removes known analytics cookies.
