# AVEVA Intouch 2023R2 - News

Jose Manuel Luque May 05, 2024

Aveva AVEVA Intouch Intouch SCADA SCADA

![AVEVA Intouch 2023R2 - News](./assets/images/blog-aveva-intouch-2023r2-novedades/IG_SVG-1-1.png)

![](./assets/images/blog-aveva-intouch-2023r2-novedades/svg.png)

One of the new features of the new version of System Platform 2023R2, which includes AVEVA Intouch, is the import of \*.svg files  
**Scalable Vector Graphics**: _**Scalable Vector Graphics**_ (**[SVG](https://www.w3.org/Graphics/SVG/)**)  
Did you know? Well, look at this marvel...

Either from the menu or by clicking the icon, we click import and select our image. I searched for an open-source library and downloaded the following.

![](./assets/images/blog-aveva-intouch-2023r2-novedades/IG_SVG-3-2.png)

Once imported, we have all the objects that make up our image. You can now create the animations you need and reuse the resources.

![](./assets/images/blog-aveva-intouch-2023r2-novedades/IG_SVG-2-1.png)

Instantiate your object and get to work... This functionality is also present in Plant SCADA 2023 R2, and I haven't checked, but I suppose it's the same with AVEVA Edge.

![](./assets/images/blog-aveva-intouch-2023r2-novedades/IG_SVG-1-1-1024x614.png)
