# Other SCADAs

Articles about other SCADA systems: FIX32/iFix, Infilink, TopKapi and Zenon.

In this section you will find articles about **other SCADA systems** on the market: **FIX32/iFix** (GE Fanuc/Emerson), **Infilink HMI**, **TopKapi** (AREAL) and **COPA-DATA Zenon**.

**FIX32** was one of the first SCADAs for Windows, evolving into **iFix** under GE Fanuc (now Emerson). **Zenon** by COPA-DATA is a modern platform that combines HMI and SCADA with a "zero engineering" philosophy and extensive connectivity. **TopKapi** by AREAL is a French SCADA used in infrastructure and utilities.

Each has its own characteristics: iFix with its process database system and Fix Background, Zenon with its no-programming parametrization, and TopKapi with its remote management focus. Here we document the configurations and tests we have performed with each one.
