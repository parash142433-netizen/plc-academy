Articles about CitectScada and Vijeo Citect.

**Schneider Electric** offers several SCADA solutions, with **CitectScada** and **Vijeo Citect** being the most prominent. CitectScada is a robust system for industrial process supervision with a powerful proprietary scripting language (**Cicode**) and support for multiple communication protocols.

CitectScada stands out for its flexible architecture (standalone, client/server, distributed), its I/O driver engine for communication with Schneider and third-party controllers, and its database integration capabilities via ODBC and Cicode/VBA. Vijeo Citect is the version integrated into Schneider's automation suite.

In this section you will find guides on driver configuration, email sending, database data logging and simulation with PLC Unity Pro.
